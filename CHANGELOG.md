# Changelog

## 2.0.0

First release as **NitroSystem II**, a continuation of [qiangqiang101's NitroSystem](https://github.com/qiangqiang101/NitroSystem) (1.x, internally *FnFNitro*).

- Nitro is now a garage upgrade: **Garage > Upgrades > Custom upgrade > Engine > Nitrous Kit** (Stock, Stage 1, Stage 2, Stage 3), bought per owned car. No kit, no nitro.
- Stage prices (3 000 / 6 000 / 12 000 CR) and boosts (1.2x / 1.4x / 1.6x) are configurable in the ini; the menu entry can be moved to another area.
- The NOS gauge frame is hidden on cars without a kit.
- Custom upgrade icons for the kit and its three stages.
- New effects while nitro burns: wider field of view, pulsing blue vignette, NFS Unbound style speed lines (each can be switched off).
- Default tank is 5 seconds; the strength setting is replaced by the per-stage boost.
- The DLL is now `NitroSystemII.dll`, the settings file `NitroSystemII.ini` and the custom sound folder `NitroSystemII`. To update from FnFNitro: delete `FnFNitro.dll`, then rename `FnFNitro.ini` to `NitroSystemII.ini` (and the `FnFNitro` sound folder to `NitroSystemII`).
- Update checks through the platform (the title screen tells you about a new release).
